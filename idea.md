# GalleryAI: AI-Powered Gallery Cleaner

## Problem Statement
Mobile storage issues are prevalent among Android and iPhone users. Galleries often become overloaded with unwanted content, such as memes shared via WhatsApp, outdated documents, or miscellaneous photos that accumulate over time.

## Solution Overview
GalleryAI is an AI-based gallery cleaner app designed to intelligently identify and suggest deletion of unwanted photos, helping users reclaim storage space while respecting their preferences and privacy.

## Key Features
1. **Onboarding Preferences**: During initial setup, users select categories they wish to protect from deletion, such as family photos, important documents, etc. The app provides a predefined list including memes, car photos, and other common categories.

2. **AI-Powered Scanning**: The app analyzes images in the gallery using AI to understand their content. It identifies photos based on user-defined preferences and suggests deletions accordingly.

3. **User Confirmation**: Before any deletion, the app seeks user confirmation, ensuring no accidental loss of important data.

4. **Privacy Controls**: Users can designate specific folders as private, preventing AI from scanning those images to maintain privacy.

## Market Differentiation
While existing apps focus on finding duplicates or blurry images, GalleryAI uniquely leverages AI to recognize and categorize photo content, providing a more intelligent and personalized cleaning experience.

## Potential Challenges
- Ensuring AI accuracy in image recognition.
- Balancing battery and performance impact during scanning.
- Implementing robust privacy measures.
